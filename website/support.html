Restore My Day Pro

Open Settings > My Day Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Manage or cancel your subscription

On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > My Day Pro.

iCloud sync

In My Day: Settings > iCloud > Sync with iCloud. You must be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone.

Your steps from Apple Health

Turn them on or off in My Day: Settings > Show my steps, or in the Health app > Sharing > Apps > My Day.
